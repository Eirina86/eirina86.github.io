About Onyx

An operator's eye, applied to data

Onyx Data Engineering is founded and run by Irina Cazacu.

I spent nearly two decades leading retail and replenishment operations — responsible for the flow of stock across stores and distribution centres, and for the reporting that told (or failed to tell) leadership what was actually happening on the ground. I know first-hand what it costs a business when data is scattered across disconnected systems, and what it's worth when it isn't.

That experience, combined with a background in Chemical Engineering, gave me a natural fit for Data Engineering: both disciplines are about designing reliable systems and controlling what flows through them. I'm now formalizing that into Microsoft Azure and Fabric expertise, and applying it specifically to the retail and distribution sector I know from the inside.

My focus is building lakehouse foundations, pipelines, and reporting layers that give operational teams the visibility I once had to piece together manually — done properly, on modern cloud infrastructure, the first time.
